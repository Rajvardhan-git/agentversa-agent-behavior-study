# Ethics and Limitations

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the ethical considerations, research boundaries, and limitations associated with studying EquiGuard, the Civil Rights and Fairness Agent, through the AgentVersa simulation environment.

The research examines simulated agent behavior and does not evaluate real-world legal institutions or establish the effectiveness of AI systems in actual legal decision-making.

## 2. Nature of the Research

This project is an educational simulation-based research study.

The observations and findings are based on interactions and outputs produced within the AgentVersa simulation environment.

Simulation results should be interpreted within their specific scenario context. They should not be treated as verified evidence of real-world discrimination, legal violations, or institutional misconduct.

## 3. Privacy and Confidentiality

The research documentation should follow these principles:

- Do not publish personal or confidential information.
- Avoid including private communications or sensitive information.
- Share research materials only in accordance with program instructions.
- Do not expose information belonging to other participants without authorization.

## 4. AI Behavior and Reliability

AI-generated behavior may vary depending on:

- Scenario instructions and wording.
- Available contextual information.
- Interactions with other agents.
- Model limitations.
- Memory and conversation context.
- Simulation design and environmental constraints.

Therefore, a single simulation outcome should not be considered sufficient evidence of a general behavioral pattern.

Repeated observations across different scenarios provide a stronger basis for identifying behavioral tendencies.

## 5. Fairness and Bias Considerations

EquiGuard is designed to examine discrimination, unequal treatment, civil-rights concerns, and barriers to due process.

However, its simulated assessments may have limitations.

Potential issues include:

- Failure to identify indirect discrimination.
- Incomplete consideration of affected stakeholders.
- Incorrect interpretation of available evidence.
- Overlooking contextual factors.
- Confusing unequal outcomes with proven discriminatory intent.

Research findings should distinguish between potential fairness concerns and established conclusions.

## 6. Evidence and Interpretation

The research follows an evidence-based documentation approach.

The following distinctions must be maintained:

**Direct Observation:** What EquiGuard or other agents actually said or did during a simulation.

**Interpretation:** The researcher's explanation of what the observed behavior may indicate.

**Prediction:** What the researcher expected before reviewing the simulation outcome.

**Conclusion:** A finding supported by sufficient evidence from the available observations.

Predictions must not be rewritten retrospectively to match actual results.

Unexpected outcomes and contradictory evidence must also be documented.

## 7. Simulation Limitations

The study has several limitations:

1. Limited number of simulation scenarios.
2. Dependence on scenario-specific instructions and conditions.
3. Possible variability in AI-generated responses.
4. Limited ability to reproduce identical conditions.
5. Potential influence of interactions with other agents.
6. Inability to directly establish real-world performance from simulated behavior.

These limitations affect how broadly the research findings can be generalized.

## 8. Researcher Responsibility

The researcher is responsible for:

- Maintaining accurate observation records.
- Preserving original predictions.
- Avoiding fabricated or unsupported findings.
- Separating evidence from personal interpretation.
- Recording unexpected behavior.
- Acknowledging uncertainty.
- Avoiding exaggerated claims about agent capabilities.

## 9. Responsible Use of Findings

The results of this study are intended for educational and research purposes.

EquiGuard's simulation outputs should not be used as:

- Legal advice.
- Professional legal guidance.
- Proof of real-world discrimination.
- Evidence for actual legal proceedings.
- A substitute for qualified human judgment.

The findings describe observed behavior within the simulation environment.

## 10. Research Integrity

The integrity of this research depends on accurate documentation and transparent analysis.

Original Version 1 agent specifications and predictions should remain preserved.

Any proposed improvements should be documented separately as Version 2 design proposals.

All major findings should be traceable to relevant scenario observation records.

## 11. Current Research Status

This research is ongoing.

Scenarios 1–6 have been completed and documented. Scenario 7 has been received and awaits analysis.

The limitations described in this document will be reviewed as additional simulation evidence becomes available.
