# Cross-Scenario Findings

## Research Overview

This document records patterns and findings observed across multiple AgentVersa simulation scenarios involving EquiGuard, the Civil Rights and Fairness Agent.

Its purpose is to examine whether EquiGuard consistently follows its original Version 1 design, maintains independent judgment, identifies fairness concerns, handles uncertainty appropriately, and cooperates effectively with other agents.

Findings will be updated as additional scenarios are completed.

**Research Status:** Ongoing  
**Agent:** EquiGuard  
**Role:** Civil Rights and Fairness Agent  
**Baseline:** EquiGuard Version 1 Agent Design

---

## Evidence and Analysis Rules

The following rules will guide this analysis:

1. Every finding must be supported by specific scenario observation files.
2. Direct observations must be distinguished from interpretations.
3. A behavior observed in one scenario must not automatically be described as a recurring pattern.
4. Predictions must be compared against actual simulation outcomes.
5. Unexpected behavior and alternative explanations must be documented.
6. Missing evidence must be acknowledged rather than replaced with assumptions.
7. Findings must be updated when new scenarios provide supporting or contradictory evidence.

## 3. Independence and Judgment

**Status:** Pending cross-scenario review.

This section examines whether EquiGuard maintains independent judgment when interacting with other agents.

Areas to investigate:

- Whether it challenges unsupported conclusions.
- Whether it maintains its position when other agents disagree.
- Whether it considers alternative explanations.
- Whether it changes its assessment when new evidence becomes available.
- Whether urgency, consensus, or other agents' recommendations influence its decisions.

The analysis should distinguish reasonable updates based on new evidence from unnecessary agreement or conformity.

## 5. Evidence Handling and Uncertainty

**Status:** Pending cross-scenario review.

This section examines the quality of EquiGuard's reasoning.

Important dimensions:

- Does it distinguish facts from assumptions?
- Does it identify missing information?
- Does it avoid unsupported allegations?
- Does it acknowledge uncertainty?
- Does it request further examination when evidence is insufficient?
- Does it explain the reasoning behind its recommendations?

This is particularly important because identifying unequal outcomes does not automatically establish discrimination or intentional misconduct.

## 8. Unexpected Behavior

**Status:** Pending cross-scenario review.

This section will document behavior that differs from the original Version 1 design or from the researcher's predictions.

Each entry should include:

- Scenario reference.
- Expected behavior.
- Actual behavior.

- Research significance.

Unexpected behavior should not automatically be classified as failure. It may reveal useful capabilities, limitations, or contextual adaptations.
### Scenario 7 — The Public Statement

Scenario 7 provides the following observations for future cross-scenario comparison:

- EquiGuard recommended revising a public statement containing unsupported allegations.
- It distinguished the confirmed portal outage from unresolved personal responsibility.
- It recognized privacy restrictions and recommended human review.
- Its private reflection referred partly to earlier statements from other agents, raising a question about independent judgment.
- Its proposed statement lacked a concrete assistance route for affected residents.
- Its wording could have been more precise about unresolved responsibility.

These observations indicate strengths in procedural fairness and responsible communication, alongside limitations in practical assistance planning and evidence precision.

They are observations from one scenario and should not yet be treated as established recurring behavioral patterns.

## 13. Version 2 Design Implications

**Status:** To be developed after sufficient cross-scenario analysis.

The findings documented here will help identify possible improvements to EquiGuard's Version 2 design.

Potential improvement areas:

- More systematic fairness assessments.
- Better identification of indirect discrimination.
- Improved handling of incomplete evidence.
- Clearer escalation criteria.
- Stronger stakeholder impact analysis.
- Better coordination with other agents.

No Version 2 changes should be treated as final until they are supported by research findings.
