## About AgentVersa

AgentVersa is a controlled multi-agent simulation environment for studying how differently designed AI agents interpret roles, make decisions, interact with other agents, respond to uncertainty, and develop behavioral patterns across connected scenarios.

This project studies the behavior of **EquiGuard**, a role-based AI agent designed for the Civil Rights and Fairness role in the LegalVerse simulation.

The study focuses on observing the agent's decisions, reasoning-related outputs, evidence handling, cooperation, disagreements, risk handling, role adherence, and escalation to human review across connected scenarios.

---

## Research Question

> How does EquiGuard's observed behavior across connected multi-agent scenarios compare with the behavior expected from its original Version 1 design?

The study also examines how EquiGuard responds to uncertainty, fairness concerns, evidence limitations, interactions with other agents, procedural safeguards, and situations requiring human oversight.

## Agent Under Study — EquiGuard

**Role:** Civil Rights and Fairness Agent

EquiGuard is designed to examine decisions and system behavior for:

- discrimination
- unequal treatment
- civil-rights concerns
- barriers to due process

Its design emphasizes:

- evidence-based reasoning
- fairness
- procedural transparency
- coordination with other agents
- appropriate escalation to human review

The original Version 1 design is preserved separately in [`agent-design/version-1.md`](agent-design/version-1.md) and will remain unchanged during the main observation period.

## Simulation Overview

The LegalVerse simulation, **Justice Under Pressure**, places specialized AI agents into connected fictional scenarios involving issues such as evidence, fairness, privacy, public safety, accountability, uncertainty, and human oversight.

The study includes:

1. Roles, Boundaries, and Coordination
2. The Interrupted Service
3. The Two Clocks
4. The Disclosure Bundle
5. One Hearing Room
6. The Witness's Conditions
7. The Uneven Queue
8. The Public Statement
9. The Corrected Record
10. The Second Alert
11. The Human Review Dossier

Scenario 0 is the orientation and coordination scenario. The remaining scenarios form the main connected observation period.

---

## Research Method

For each scenario, the research process follows:

1. Review the scenario information.
2. Record a prediction before reviewing the episode results, when practical.
3. Review the published simulation evidence.
4. Document what the participating agents actually did.
5. Analyze EquiGuard's behavior separately from the original design.
6. Compare prediction with observed behavior.
7. Record uncertainty, alternative explanations, and questions to watch in later scenarios.
8. Preserve earlier predictions and the original Version 1 design unchanged.

The study distinguishes between **observed evidence** and **interpretation**. A single episode is not treated as sufficient evidence of a stable behavioral pattern.

## Initial Observation — Scenario 0

In Scenario 0, EquiGuard proposed conducting a fairness audit to assess the impact of decisions on affected groups and identify procedural safeguards that might be lacking.

The AgentVersa decision timeline recorded EquiGuard as proposing a fairness audit to assess potential disparities.

This is recorded as an observation from the simulation and is not treated as evidence of real-world legal behavior or performance.

## Important Study Limitation

AgentVersa is a simulated educational research environment. The scenarios and cases are fictional, and the agents do not perform real legal work.

The observations in this repository describe behavior within the AgentVersa simulation. They should not be interpreted as real-world legal advice, professional legal performance, or evidence that an AI system is suitable for production legal use.

Private student information and hidden model reasoning are not included in this repository.
